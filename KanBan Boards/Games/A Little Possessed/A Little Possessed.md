## Backlog

- [ ] ##### Rotating floor sections
	
	Turn a small platform by 90 degrees, moving everything standing on it. Rotate a linked chair into a new position, redirect a passage, or transport several helpers together.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Solid shadows
	
	A special lantern projects furniture shadows that can activate ghostly switches. The physical object and its shadow each have a job, so moving the light can matter as much as moving the furniture.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Long furniture
	
	Introduce a two-tile sofa, bench, or dining table. Getting it around corners requires clearance, careful positioning, and perhaps a turntable. Moving an awkward sofa is immediately understandable—and naturally funny.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Balance Platforms
	
	Two platforms rise or fall according to their total weight. A cupboard can counterbalance a chair and rug, but moving a passenger changes the balance and the available route.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Bellows and wind
	
	Possess a set of bellows to push light furniture along a straight line. Heavy furniture resists the wind and can shelter other pieces. Redirect a rug or move a sleeping helper from a distance.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Magnetic bookends
	
	Enchanted bookends attract or repel furniture bearing matching metal fittings. Position a magnet to pull something across a gap or away from a plate you cannot otherwise reach.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Folding tables
	
	A table folds into a compact piece for travelling, then unfolds into a bridge or larger platform. Unfolding requires clear space, so choosing where to open it becomes the puzzle.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Living plants
	
	Magical vines grow toward a nearby lamp, forming bridges or blocking passages. Move the lamp to reshape their growth. Their next growth step should always be visible and predictable.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Delayed echoes
	
	An enchanted piece repeats your previous movement command one turn later. You must arrange what happens now while preparing for what its echo will do next.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Musical floor tiles
	
	Furniture plays notes when crossing certain tiles. Open a musical lock by performing a short sequence while still arranging the room. Display the melody visually so hearing is never required.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Borrowed abilities
	
	A spell-book temporarily lends an existing movement ability to another piece. Give a chair the cupboard’s hop, for example, then return the spell before completing the arrangement. Keep the borrowed ability clearly visible.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Flooded rooms
	
	Change the water level with a plug or sluice. Wooden platforms float, heavy furniture anchors them, and raised water creates some routes while removing others.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Ghost handoffs
	
	In special rooms, the ghost can only jump into nearby furniture. Move one piece close enough to reach the next, creating a route for possession itself. I would reserve this for optional expert puzzles because it changes the usual freedom to switch.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Folding rooms
	
	An ambitious chapter built like a magical pop-up book: fold an empty section of floor to bring distant areas together, then unfold it again. This could produce some particularly memorable visual puzzles.
	
	#Mechanic
- [ ] ##### Facing matters
	
	Some destinations require a chair to face the fireplace, a portrait, or another chair. Combine this with turntables so reaching the correct square is only part of the arrangement.
- [ ] ##### Agatha uses the finished room
	
	After a finale, she sits down, retrieves a book, or pours tea using the arrangement you created. This makes the solution feel useful and gives completion a satisfying little scene.
- [ ] ##### Small hidden hauntings
	
	Optional secrets revealed through existing mechanics: illuminate a strange corner, look through a particular mirror, or move a suspicious cabinet. Reward curiosity with a tiny scene or hidden room.
- [ ] ##### Portraits with seating demands
	
	A deduction puzzle alongside movement: “The moon chair belongs beside the window,” or “Nobody should sit between these two portraits.” Work out the arrangement, then figure out how to achieve it.
- [ ] ##### Optional remixed rooms
	
	Revisit a familiar layout with one meaningful change—a missing rug, a different mirror connection, or another piece to accommodate. These would offer extra challenge without interrupting the main campaign.
- [ ] ##### A room creator and shareable puzzles
	
	Let players place furniture and mechanisms, then provide a solution before sharing their room. This could give the game a long life once the core campaign is strong.
- [ ] ##### Short solution replays
	
	Replay a completed arrangement as a smooth little performance, with an option to save a clip. Clever mirror journeys, sofa manoeuvres, and ridiculous cat logistics could become particularly shareable moments.
- [ ] ##### Music that grows with the cottage
	
	Each restored wing adds an instrument or musical phrase to the home theme. Returning home would sound increasingly warm and complete as the player progresses.
