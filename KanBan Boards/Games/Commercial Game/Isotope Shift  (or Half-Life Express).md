**Brief Description**: "Overcooked meets Nuclear Option" You run a high-tech waste management depot where radioactive isotopes decay, fuse, and mutate in real time. You must manually route, cool, and dispose of decaying elements before they melt through the floor or detonate.

**Core Purpose of the game** -> Prevent catastrophic nuclear meltdowns while processing volatile radioactive elements under extreme time pressure to earn credits and upgrade your facility.

### Where the Fun Comes From

- **High-Stakes Priority Juggling**: Every block on screen is an active fuse counting down to zero. The core thrill comes from split-second prioritization—deciding whether to cool an overheating uranium block, fuse two plutonium blocks to save space, or dump a critical reactor core before it explodes.
- **Satisfying Chain Reactions**: Grouping blocks strategically allows one fuse/fusion to trigger an automated cascade that clears half your board instantly. Setting up a multi-step chain reaction creates a massive burst of dopamine and visual feedback (glowing screen shake, particle bursts, high-score popups).
- **Controlled Chaos & Clutch Saves**: The game naturally builds tension as conveyor belts speed up. The best moments happen when a catastrophe is imminent (three blocks on 1 second remaining), and you pull off a rapid series of tractor-beam throws and coolant dumps to stabilize the room right on the brink of failure.

### Detailed Core Game Loop

#### Win Condition

##### Per Level

Fill the shift's quota meter by processing and launching enough stabilized high-tier isotopes down the Delivery Chute before time runs out.
##### Campaign/Game End

Complete all facility sectors (e.g., _Sector 1: Standard Lab_ $\rightarrow$ _Sector 5: Core Reactor Floor_). Reaching the end unlocks **Endless Overtime Mode**, where players compete on global Steam leader boards for highest score before inevitable failure.

#### How the Player Loses (Fail State)

1. **Facility Critical Mass (Meltdown)**
		Every unhandled explosion or un-cooled leak adds heat to the facility’s central **Radiation/Meltdown Bar**. If this bar reaches 100%, the facility locks down, the shift immediately terminates in a nuclear burst, and you must restart or retry the level.
2. **Quota Failure**
		If the shift timer hits 0:00 and you haven't filled the required quota chute, your contract is terminated due to under-performance.

#### Where the Gameplay Stakes Come From

- **Calculated Risk:** You can let a cheap block explode to save a rare, expensive block—sacrificing a chunk of your Meltdown Bar as a strategic trade-off.
- **Escalating Danger:** The closer you get to your quota, the faster conveyors spawn incoming volatile blocks, forcing you to balance speed with careful handling right up to the finish line.
