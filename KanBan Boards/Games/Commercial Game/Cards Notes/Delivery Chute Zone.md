- **Design Objective:** Create an environmental zone where players submit completed blocks to earn points.
    
- **Steps to Take:**
    
    - [ ] Designate a visually distinct area on the floor marked as the "Delivery Chute."
        
    - [ ] Detect when a block enters the chute boundaries.
        
    - [ ] Remove the block from the game field.
        
    - [ ] Calculate and award point value based on the submitted block's tier.
        
- **Definition of Done:** Dropping a block into the Delivery Chute removes the block from play and adds score to the total points earned.
