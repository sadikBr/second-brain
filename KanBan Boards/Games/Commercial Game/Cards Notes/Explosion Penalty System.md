- **Design Objective:** Establish a clear negative consequence when a block's timer hits zero.
    
- **Steps to Take:**
    
    - [ ] Detect when any block's timer reaches zero seconds.
        
    - [ ] Trigger a visual explosion effect at the block's current location.
        
    - [ ] Remove the expired block from the board.
        
    - [ ] Send a penalty signal to increase the overall room hazard level.
        
- **Definition of Done:** Letting a block timer reach zero causes an explosion effect, removes the block, and increases the overall facility risk level.
